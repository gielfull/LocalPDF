# Code of Conduct

LocalPDF follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and respectful, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep
discussions about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks aren't tolerated in
issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer,
[@gielfull](https://github.com/gielfull), privately. Reports are handled confidentially.
The maintainer may remove comments or block participants who violate this code, as
described in the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
